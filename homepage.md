# SHADESUK --- Homepage Architecture

**Status:** Approved\
**Direction:** Editorial Commerce\
**Primary objective:** Build a premium eyewear storefront where brand
perception and product discovery reinforce each other.

## 1. Homepage North Star

The homepage should feel like entering a modern eyewear campaign, not
browsing a generic ecommerce template.

The experience combines: - Fashion editorial presentation - Premium
ecommerce merchandising - Strong product visibility - Minimal
interface - Controlled visual pacing - Clear conversion paths

Core principle:

> **Editorial content creates desire. Product exposure creates intent.
> Navigation removes friction.**

The customer should repeatedly encounter products while scrolling, but
the experience must never feel like an aggressive sales catalogue.

## 2. Core Homepage Rhythm

**CAMPAIGN → PRODUCT → EDITORIAL → PRODUCT → CATEGORY → PRODUCT →
PRODUCT STORY → PRODUCT → TRUST → SHOP**

The customer should generally never move through a long content-only
section without a meaningful product or shopping opportunity nearby.

This is the deliberate low-bounce / high-discovery strategy.

## 3. Section Architecture

### 01 --- Header

Purpose: immediate navigation without competing with campaign imagery.

Desktop: - SHADESUK logo - SHOP - NEW ARRIVALS - BEST SELLERS -
COLLECTIONS - ABOUT - Search - Account - Bag

Rules: - No Home in primary navigation. - Logo links to Home. - Keep
navigation visually quiet. - Avoid oversized navigation bars. - Avoid
unnecessary iconography. - Sticky behaviour may be used, but must be
subtle.

### 02 --- Hero Campaign

Purpose: establish brand perception within the first few seconds.

Use one exceptional campaign/product image, preferably authentic
supplied imagery.

Avoid generic stock imagery, obvious AI-generated models, synthetic
environments and excessive effects.

Copy should be extremely short.

Example direction:

**SHADES FOR EVERY ANGLE.**

CTAs: - SHOP MEN - SHOP WOMEN

The hero must communicate: 1. This is an eyewear brand. 2. The brand is
premium and contemporary. 3. The customer can start shopping
immediately.

### 03 --- Immediate Product Exposure

Purpose: move directly from brand desire into product discovery.

Example heading:

**THE SHADES PEOPLE ARE WEARING**

Use a curated product presentation: - 4 products desktop - 2 products
mobile - Strong product imagery - Minimal product metadata

Card hierarchy: 1. Image 2. Product name 3. Price 4. Sale information
where applicable

CTA: **SHOP ALL SHADES**

### 04 --- Editorial Brand Moment

Purpose: give the brand personality and visual breathing room.

Use a large campaign/editorial image and one concise brand statement.

Example:

**BUILT AROUND THE WAY YOU SEE THE WORLD.**

No large paragraph. The section should feel like a fashion campaign
rather than an About Us block.

### 05 --- Shop by Gender

Purpose: provide an immediate route into relevant products.

Three visual discovery areas: - MEN - WOMEN - UNISEX

Each uses campaign/product imagery and a simple CTA: - SHOP MEN → - SHOP
WOMEN → - SHOP UNISEX →

These are editorial category tiles, not decorative cards.

### 06 --- Best Sellers

Purpose: reduce decision friction and surface proven products.

Heading direction:

**THE MOST WANTED**

Use a curated product grid or controlled horizontal rail with 4--6
products desktop and an appropriate mobile presentation.

CTA: **VIEW BEST SELLERS →**

### 07 --- Product Story

Purpose: make one product feel important rather than treating every
product as an interchangeable catalogue item.

Structure: - Large editorial/product image - Product name - One-line
description - Price - SHOP THIS STYLE →

One product gets a visual moment. This creates perceived curation and
elevates the catalogue.

### 08 --- Shop by Shape

Purpose: help customers discover eyewear through a natural product
attribute.

Heading:

**FIND YOUR FRAME**

Potential shapes: - Aviator - Rectangle - Square - Oval - Cat Eye -
Rimless - Other shapes supported by the actual catalogue

Use frame/product imagery. Keep the section clean and scannable.

### 09 --- New Arrivals

Purpose: create another commercial discovery point and give returning
visitors a reason to browse.

Heading:

**JUST IN**

Show latest products, recommended at 4 products desktop and 2 products
mobile.

CTA: **VIEW NEW ARRIVALS →**

### 10 --- Trust / Service Information

Purpose: remove purchase objections without turning the homepage into a
generic ecommerce benefits section.

Do not use a large four-icon feature row.

Use three restrained information blocks:

**SHIPPING**\
Orders are shipped internationally with a minimum expected delivery
timeframe of 2 weeks.

**PAYMENT**\
Secure card payments.

**TRACKING**\
Every order can be tracked.

Final wording will follow approved policy content.

### 11 --- Final Editorial CTA

Purpose: provide one final route into the catalogue.

Use a large campaign/product image with minimal copy.

Example:

**FIND YOUR FRAME.**

CTA:

**SHOP ALL SHADES →**

The section should feel like the conclusion of a campaign rather than
another sales banner.

## 5. Product Exposure Strategy

Product visibility is a core conversion requirement.

Sequence:

**Hero / Campaign → Product Discovery → Editorial → Gender Discovery →
Best Sellers → Product Story → Shape Discovery → New Arrivals → Trust →
Final Shop CTA**

Customers repeatedly encounter products while maintaining visual
variety.

## 6. Avoiding Product Fatigue

More product exposure does not mean showing a product grid after every
section.

Use different merchandising formats: - Curated product grid - Horizontal
product rail - Single-product editorial - Product + image composition -
Collection discovery - Shape discovery - New arrivals - Best sellers

## 7. Scroll & Interaction Rules

-   Products can load progressively as customers move through the page.
-   Avoid aggressive infinite scroll that makes the footer difficult to
    reach.
-   Use load-more or controlled progressive loading where appropriate.
-   Desktop may use a secondary product image on hover.
-   Mobile must use touch-native interactions.
-   Essential information must not depend on hover.
-   Animation should be limited to subtle image transitions, hover
    states, section reveals and drawer transitions.

## 8. Responsive Architecture

### Desktop

-   Large campaign imagery
-   Editorial whitespace
-   4-column product grids
-   Large product storytelling
-   Wide gender/category compositions

### Tablet

Adapt grid density, image ratios, navigation spacing and section
proportions.

### Mobile

Deliberately redesign rather than simply compress desktop.

Priorities: 1. Product visibility 2. Easy navigation 3. Thumb-friendly
interaction 4. Fast discovery 5. Clear CTAs 6. Simple product cards 7.
Fast checkout access

Recommended product grid: **2 columns**.

## 9. Content Requirements

Before final production: - Approved campaign imagery - Product imagery -
Product names - Product prices - Collection names - Brand statement -
Promotional offer, if any - Shipping messaging - Payment messaging -
Tracking messaging - Authentic social proof, once available

Do not build the homepage around placeholder AI copy or synthetic
lifestyle imagery.

## 10. Anti-AI-Slop Rules

Do not use: - Generic gradient hero sections - Glassmorphism - Floating
blobs - Excessive rounded cards - Fake 3D objects - Decorative sparkle
effects - Gold-on-black luxury clichés - Fake testimonials - Fake
statistics - Generic icon rows - Excessive carousel usage - Random
asymmetry - Excessive text animation - Cursor gimmicks - Unnecessary
parallax - Overloaded announcement bars - Popup-heavy first impressions

Every section must have a clear reason to exist.

If a section does not improve brand perception, product discovery,
product understanding, trust, navigation or conversion, remove it.

## 11. Homepage Success Criteria

Within the first viewport, the customer understands:

**What ShadesUK sells → what the brand feels like → where to shop.**

During scrolling, the customer repeatedly encounters:

**Products → categories → products → product stories → products.**

Before leaving, the customer has clear routes into: - Men - Women -
Unisex - Best Sellers - New Arrivals - All Shades

Overall the page must feel **curated, premium, contemporary and
product-led**.

## 12. Homepage North Star

> **Make the customer want the brand, then make discovering a product
> effortless.**

The homepage should sell the world of ShadesUK first, while continuously
giving the customer something worth buying.
