# SHADESUK

**Mode:** Persuade on campaign surfaces. Operate on catalogue, bag, checkout, account, and tracking.

**Product:** SHADESUK is a modern UK eyewear ecommerce storefront. The interface should feel like an eyewear label first and a shop second.

**Audience:** Customers choosing sunglasses. They need product clarity, fit confidence, and a low-friction guest purchase.

**Locked principle:** Make the customer want the brand, then make discovering a product effortless.

**Brand rule:** Logo is the brand signature. UI is quiet luxury.

**Non-negotiables:**

- Deep Navy `#0B1C2D` is the functional brand colour.
- Warm Off-White `#F7F6F2` is the page background.
- Near Black `#111111` is body text.
- Silver is identity only, never a general UI accent.
- One grotesk family. No decorative font mixing.
- Guest checkout and guest order tracking.
- No fake reviews, scarcity, ratings, or AI imagery.
- No gradients, glassmorphism, parallax, or scroll-jacking.
- WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Approved SHADESUK specification files are the source of truth.

**Out of scope for later phases:** Homepage, product surfaces, search experience, cart, checkout, and account functionality.
