# SHADESUK visual system

Locked from the approved SHADESUK specifications. Do not invent a replacement world.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | `#0B1C2D` | Primary actions, brand moments |
| Warm Off-White | `#F7F6F2` | Page background |
| Soft Stone | `#E9E7E1` | Secondary surfaces, dividers |
| Near Black | `#111111` | Body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Contrast surfaces |

Silver exists in the logo only. No metallic UI treatments.

## Type

Single family: Inter. Roles: Display, H1, H2, H3, Body, Small, Micro.

Hierarchy comes from size, weight, line-height, spacing, and position.

## Space

`4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 80 96 120`

Large desktop gains whitespace, not density.

## Layout

- Page grid: 4 / 8 / 12 columns
- Product grid: 2 / 3 / 4 columns (never 5–6)
- Breakpoints: `<768`, `768–1199`, `1200+`, `1440+`
- Open layouts. No card-on-card. Square corners. No excessive shadow.

## Motion

Fast, subtle, functional. Duration token respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Controls

Primary: navy field, white text. Secondary: light field, navy border. Tertiary: text. Comfortable 44px touch targets. Visible focus.
